The actual `Primary_Category` and `Matched_Categories` values were not included in the export, so I cannot reproduce the library’s exact folder names. ### 1. Most important new findings

1. **Immune dysfunction is now supported by the largest CFC-specific cohort to date.** In 56 molecularly confirmed individuals, 32% had increased infection susceptibility, 25% autoimmune manifestations, and substantial rates of lymphopenia and low immunoglobulins. The study also suggests gene-specific patterns: BRAF variants with T-cell lymphopenia, and MAP2K1 variants with monocytosis, reduced naïve/memory B cells, and hypogammaglobulinemia. Routine immunologic assessment should therefore be considered a clinically actionable CFC-care issue. ([pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40692796/?utm_source=openai))

2. **Cancer-risk evidence has become more nuanced.** The CFC pooled analysis of 690 reported individuals found elevated overall cancer and acute lymphoblastic leukemia standardized-incidence ratios, with risk apparently concentrated in early childhood; however, ascertainment limitations and only 11 cancers mean the estimates remain imprecise. Conversely, a genomic-ascertainment study found no sufficient evidence of increased cancer prevalence among adult CFC-heterozygotes, supporting age-stratified—not generalized lifelong—risk interpretation. ([pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40196938/?utm_source=openai))

3. **Neurobehavioral needs are more clearly defined.** A caregiver cohort of 61 people with CFC found challenging behavior in 77%, most prominent in late childhood/adolescence and associated with sleep disturbance, pain interference, sensory differences, epilepsy, and adaptive-function level. ([pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40387217/?utm_source=openai))

4. **CFC neurobiology has gained two complementary BRAF-model studies.** In BRAF-mutant mice and human cortical spheroids, ERK hyperactivation in astrocytes was linked to calcium dysregulation, reactive astrogliosis, and memory impairment; astrocytic ERK or calcium modulation rescued phenotypes in mice. A separate CFC Braf^Q241R^ model indicates that mature-neuron MAPK dysregulation can produce learning/activity abnormalities even without developmental malformations. ([pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39964758/?utm_source=openai))

5. **The update expands practical phenotype surveillance** across dermatology, feeding/GI, behavior, and rare tumor-like presentations—including MAP2K1-associated skin pathology, granulomatous miliaria profunda, MAP2K2/CFC4 feeding and neurodevelopmental disease, and reported Langerhans cell histiocytosis in two CFC individuals. These are mostly case-level signals, not established associations. ([pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40568820/))

## 4. Recommended next-update search expansion

### Add explicit CFC gene and phenotype terms
Use the CFC umbrella terms plus gene-specific strings:

```text
("cardiofaciocutaneous syndrome" OR "cardio-facio-cutaneous syndrome" OR CFCS OR CFC)
OR
((BRAF OR MAP2K1 OR MEK1 OR MAP2K2 OR MEK2 OR KRAS) AND
 ("germline" OR "developmental disorder" OR RASopathy) AND
 (CFC OR "cardiofaciocutaneous"))
```

### Add newly high-yield clinical concept blocks

- **Immunology:** `hypogammaglobulinemia`, `immunodeficiency`, `inborn errors of immunity`, `vaccine response`, `lymphopenia`, `B cell`, `T cell`, `autoimmunity`, `recurrent infection`.
- **Cancer/hematology:** `malignancy`, `cancer predisposition`, `acute lymphoblastic leukemia`, `Langerhans cell histiocytosis`, `hepatoblastoma`, `juvenile myelomonocytic leukemia`, `clonal hematopoiesis`.
- **Neurodevelopment/behavior:** `self-injury`, `aggression`, `challenging behavior`, `autism`, `sensory processing`, `sleep disturbance`, `pain`, `epilepsy`, `white matter`, `astrocyte`.
- **Feeding/GI/growth:** `feeding dysfunction`, `oropharyngeal dysphagia`, `failure to thrive`, `growth velocity`, `short stature`, `gastrostomy`, `gastrointestinal malformation`, `intestinal duplication`.
- **Dermatology:** `miliaria profunda`, `eccrine`, `dermatopathology`, `keratosis pilaris`, `ulerythema ophryogenes`, `alopecia`, `hemangioma`, `dermoid cyst`.
- **Models/therapeutics:** `iPSC`, `cortical spheroid`, `organoid`, `BRAF Q241R`, `BRAF K499E`, `MEK inhibition`, `ERK inhibition`, `RAS/MAPK modulation`.

### Adjacent concepts to monitor, but keep separate from core CFC evidence

1. **CFC-like chromosomal disorders and diagnostic mimics**—especially deletions and ectodermal dysplasia syndromes. Maintain a separate *Differential diagnosis / phenotype mimics* tag rather than mixing them with molecularly confirmed CFC.
2. **Gene-specific CFC subtypes**—search and tag **BRAF-CFC**, **MAP2K1-CFC**, **MAP2K2-CFC/CFC4**, and **KRAS-CFC** independently, because the immunology and phenotype literature increasingly reports gene-associated differences. ([pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40692796/?utm_source=openai))
3. **Age-stratified cancer surveillance**—separate pediatric from adult evidence in future screening, because recent CFC cancer studies reach different but potentially compatible conclusions depending on ascertainment and age. ([pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40196938/?utm_source=openai))
